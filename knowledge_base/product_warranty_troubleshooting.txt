# Product Warranty and Technical Troubleshooting

## 1. Warranty Coverage
- All hardware devices include a 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- The warranty period begins on the confirmed delivery date.
- Accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized third-party modifications, and electrical surges are not covered under the standard warranty.

## 2. Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Claims
- If a product fails to power on or operate upon unboxing, it qualifies as DOA.
- DOA claims filed within 30 days of delivery receive an immediate expedited replacement unit dispatched with prepaid return shipping for the defective device.

## 3. RMA Replacement Process
1. Contact Technical Support with your Serial Number and description of the hardware fault.
2. A support engineer will initiate diagnostic verification steps.
3. If confirmed defective, an RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number and prepaid shipping label will be issued.
4. Replacement devices are dispatched within 2 business days of receipt at our repair center.

## 4. Basic Hardware Troubleshooting Steps
- **Device Not Powering On:** Verify power adapter is firmly connected, test with an alternate wall socket, and perform a 30-second hard power reset by holding the power button.
- **Connectivity / Wi-Fi Loss:** Reboot the gateway router, ensure firmware version is up to date, and hold the reset button for 10 seconds to restore factory network defaults.
