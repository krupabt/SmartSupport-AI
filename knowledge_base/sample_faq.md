# Frequently Asked Customer Questions (FAQ)

## 1. How do I change my registered email address?
To update your primary email address, navigate to **Profile Settings -> Email Address**, enter the new email, and verify the confirmation link sent to your new inbox.

## 2. Where can I download invoices and tax receipts?
Tax invoices and receipts are automatically generated in PDF format under **Billing -> Invoices** after every successful transaction. You can download or print them anytime.

## 3. Can I transfer my license to another organization?
Yes, license ownership transfers are permitted for active Enterprise and Pro accounts by opening a transfer request with our Accounts Management team.
