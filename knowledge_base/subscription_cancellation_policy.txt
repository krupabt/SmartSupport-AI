# Subscription Cancellation and Billing Policy

## 1. Plan Types & Billing Cycles
- SmartSupport AI offers Monthly and Annual subscription plans.
- Monthly subscriptions auto-renew on the same calendar day each month.
- Annual plans auto-renew every 365 days from the activation date.

## 2. Cancellation Process
- Customers can cancel their subscription at any time directly through the Billing Dashboard by clicking "Cancel Plan".
- Cancellations take effect at the end of the current paid billing cycle. You retain full access to all features until the end of the paid term.
- There are zero cancellation fees or early termination penalties.

## 3. Prorated Refunds on Annual Plans
- For Annual subscriptions cancelled within 30 days of renewal/purchase, customers may request a full refund or prorated refund for unused months.
- Monthly subscriptions do not offer partial month prorated refunds, but remain active through the final day of the cycle.

## 4. Account Downgrades & Data Retention
- Upon cancellation expiration, your account downgrades to the Free Tier.
- Ticket records and audit logs are securely archived for 12 months after downgrade and can be exported at any time.
