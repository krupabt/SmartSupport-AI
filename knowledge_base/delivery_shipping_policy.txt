# Delivery and Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Options & Delivery Estimates
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivered within 3 to 5 business days for domestic metropolitan areas, and 5 to 7 business days for regional zones.
- **Express Shipping:** Priority courier delivery within 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST ship same day.
- **International Shipping:** Global deliveries take 7 to 14 business days, subject to customs clearance in the destination country.

## 2. Order Tracking & Status
- Every shipment generates an automated Tracking Number and Carrier Link (FedEx, DHL, BlueDart, or UPS) within 12 hours of dispatch.
- Customers can track real-time parcel transit from their customer portal or by providing their order Reference ID.

## 3. Delayed, Lost, or Damaged Shipments
- **Delayed Shipments:** If a parcel has not arrived within 3 days past the estimated delivery date, our logistics team initiates an emergency carrier tracer.
- **Lost in Transit:** If the courier declares a parcel lost or there is zero tracking scan update for 7 consecutive days, we immediately dispatch a free replacement or issue a full refund at the customer's discretion.
- **Damaged on Arrival:** Customers must report physical packaging damage within 48 hours of delivery along with photographic evidence to receive immediate priority replacement.

## 4. Address Corrections
- Delivery addresses can be modified for free within 2 hours of order placement.
- Once dispatched, address rerouting requests depend on carrier availability and may incur a 24-hour delivery delay.
